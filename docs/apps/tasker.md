## How-to

### Open the Loki Patches menu

- **Tasker's own overflow menu** (the three dots at the top right of the main screen) → **Loki Patches**.
- Long press the Tasker icon on your home screen → **Loki Patches**.

The Tasky beginner screen has no overflow menu; use the long-press shortcut from there.

### Notes

- Tasker asks its licence question in more than one place. All of them are answered, and the cached status is
  Licensed, so the app stays unlocked offline and after a restart.
- Plugins such as AutoApps have their own licences and are not covered.
- Tasker is built for arm64 and armv7.
